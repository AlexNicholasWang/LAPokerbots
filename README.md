# LA Pokerbots

**UCLA's poker AI competition.** Build a bot, battle it out over 500,000+ hands, and win a share of the prize pool at the live finals.

🌐 **Website:** [lapokerbots.com](#) · 📅 **Register:** [Luma](https://lu.ma/) · ✉️ **Contact:** alexwang770@g.ucla.edu

## Winter 2027 — One-Week Sprint

| Date | Event |
|------|-------|
| **Jan 10** | Kickoff |
| **Jan 10–15** | Build & scrimmage |
| **Jan 16** | Qualifier |
| **Jan 17** | Live finals — top 8 bracket |

### Format

- **Teams of 1–4** — open eligibility, no experience required
- Bots face off over **500K+ hands** to minimize variance
- Write your bot in **Python, C++, or Java**
- Top 8 teams from the qualifier advance to a live finals bracket

### Prizes

| Place | Prize |
|-------|-------|
| 🥇 1st | $2,500 |
| 🥈 2nd | $1,000 |
| 🥉 3rd | $500 |
| 4th | $500 |

## About

LA Pokerbots brings the spirit of MIT Pokerbots to the West Coast: a week-long sprint where students design, build, and refine autonomous poker agents, then watch them compete head-to-head. Whether you're into game theory, machine learning, or just want to out-bluff your friends with code, there's a seat at the table.

Organized by students at UCLA.
